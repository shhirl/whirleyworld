About

From coaching to code.

Before data science I was a personal trainer. Now I combine my love for health and fitness with data and engineering skills. I still train friends and measure success the way a coach does: by what someone can do now that they couldn't before.

Today I'm AI Product Lead at SWISS, part of Lufthansa Group's Digital Hangar. I run the AI portfolio for our customer digital experience domain and decide which ideas get funded and which get stopped. Because I came from data science, I read every pitch from both sides: does it hold up technically, and will it survive real users, real data and real constraints?

Before that: an MSc in Data Science from Barcelona GSE, then seven years in the engine room building models, pipelines and cloud migrations. I work in English, German and Chinese.

Outside work I teach at Constructor Academy in Zurich, lead sponsorships for Women in Data Science Zurich, and speak on agentic AI. I have also taught HTML and Python to kids aged 7 to 17 with CoderDojo, which remains the hardest audience I have had to explain anything to.
